# BEAUREGARDE — Inner Monologue Thought System

**Style:** Deus Ex / Elysium Inner Monologue  
**Character:** Fragmented, Damaged, PTSD-based  
**Purpose:** Protagonist reveals her needs, fears, discoveries, and trauma through unfiltered thoughts

---

## Core Philosophy

**The protagonist's thoughts are the ONLY UI.**

No quest markers. No objective text. No status messages. No tutorial prompts.

**Everything the player needs to understand comes through her inner monologue:**
- What she needs (hunger, rest, safety)
- What she's noticing (details about world)
- What she's realizing (connections between information)
- What she's feeling (fear, confusion, determination)
- What she's remembering (fragmented trauma)

Her thoughts are:
- **Unfiltered** — Raw, not polished
- **Fragmented** — Incomplete sentences, thoughts trailing off
- **Repetitive** — Trauma means she repeats concerns
- **Non-linear** — Jumps between topics (dissociation)
- **Physical** — References her body, pain, cybernetics
- **Emotional** — Shows her state (panic, exhaustion, determination)

---

## Thought Categories

### Category 1: Survival Needs (Gameplay Mechanics Made Emotional)

**When Hungry:**
```
First Hunger Signal (Mild):
"My stomach. When did I eat last?"
"Food. I need to think about food soon."
"There's that store. I could... no. Not yet."

Moderate Hunger:
"I'm hungry. Really hungry. Can't focus."
"My hands are shaking. Is that fear or hunger?"
"How long can I go without eating? I don't... I don't remember."

Severe Hunger:
"Starving. I'm actually starving."
"Everything is distant. The world is... fading?"
"Need. Food. Now."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Hunger visible through thought *density* (more scattered when hungry)
- Hunger affects movement speed (mentioned in thoughts: "My legs are weak")
- Hunger affects decision-making (riskier thoughts when desperate)
- No hunger meter — just her experiences describing it

---

### Category 2: Fear & Hypervigilance (Trauma Response)

**Noticing Danger:**
```
Low Alert:
"That patrol. They're just routine. I can... I can ignore them."
"Someone's watching? No. No one's paying attention to me."
"My heart's racing but... it's okay. It's just The Veil."

Medium Alert:
"Bureau agents. Two blocks over. Are they looking for someone?"
"Looking for me? Don't be paranoid. But... they could be."
"I need to be careful. More careful than usual."

High Alert (Threat Detected):
"MOVE. Don't run. Don't attract attention. Just MOVE."
"They saw me. Did they? I can't tell if they saw me."
"Can't breathe. Heart's pounding. Where's an exit?"
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Her heart color (RED → YELLOW → BLUE) correlates with thought urgency
- Dangerous proximity creates scattered thoughts
- She doesn't always have accurate threat assessment (paranoia/trauma)
- Player must interpret whether threat is real or PTSD

---

### Category 3: Physical State (Cybernetics, Pain, Dissociation)

**Cybernetic Malfunction:**
```
Discovery:
"My right arm. The servo's... stiff? When did that happen?"
"Pain. Sharp. In my shoulder. Is it the modification?"
"The thing in my arm is... wrong. It's making that sound again."

Mechanical Understanding:
"The servo's out of sync. It won't respond smoothly."
"If I push too hard it'll break. If I don't use it, it'll lock up."
"I need a mechanic. But I don't have money for repairs."

Emotional:
"They did this to me. Built this into me. And now it's failing."
"Is the malfunction intentional? Tracking device that's degrading?"
"I hate this. I hate what they made me into."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Damaged cybernetics mentioned in thoughts before game shows performance drop
- Player must *listen* to recognize damage
- Getting repair becomes quest element (need money, need mechanic access)
- Physical pain impacts her emotional state

**Dissociation:**
```
When Overwhelmed:
"I'm here but I'm not. Where am I?"
"This moment doesn't feel real. Like I'm watching it happen."
"I should be scared but I'm... empty? Numb?"
"My body's moving but I'm not controlling it."

Coming Back:
"No. No focus. Be here. Be present."
"I'm safe. Right now, this second, I'm safe."
"Back in my body. Back in the moment."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Dissociation thoughts indicate extreme stress
- During dissociation, movement controls feel sluggish/weird
- Can't make good decisions while dissociated (narrative choice blocks)
- Recovery requires: rest, food, safety, community

### Category 4: Memory & Realization (Non-Linear Trauma Processing)

**Fragment Surfaces:**
```
Random Trigger:
"That smell. Antiseptic. Where is that smell coming from?"
*Trigger: Passed mechanic workshop*
"Stone walls. I remember stone walls. Underground."
"Hands holding me down. I couldn't move my arms."
"Someone said 'asset approved for deployment.'"

Piecing Together:
"Was that real? Or a dream? I can't tell anymore."
"Underground. Modification. They were... testing me?"
"I was their property. That's what they said."

Realization:
"If I was their property and they let me go... someone saved me."
"Someone got me out. But I don't remember who."
"And if they went to that trouble, they're still looking."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Memories surface unprompted, not controlled by player
- Player gains information about story through her realizing it
- Fragmented nature means player pieces it together (like player memory works)
- Nightmares trigger deeper memory surfaces

---

### Category 5: Investigation & Connections (Discovery Thinking)

**Learning About Marcus:**
```
Overhearing:
"Marcus. They said someone took Marcus."
"Who's Marcus? Why does that name... why does that name matter?"
"Someone disappeared. Supply chains. What does that mean?"

Pursuing Thread:
"Marcus worked logistics. The store owner said that."
"Supply chains. Medical supplies disappearing. Is that connected?"
"Why would someone take Marcus? What did he know?"

Making Connections:
"Marcus was investigating something. I can feel that."
"Supply patterns to institutional zone. Disappearances. Modifications."
"They're connected. It's all connected. The program took Marcus."

Understanding:
"Marcus was like me. They modified him. They took him."
"Underground. He's underground. That's where they take people."
"And I need to find him because... because they might still have him."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Player learns quest through her internal investigation
- Connections form in her mind before memory map shows them visually
- She's not told objectives — she figures out what matters
- Investigation thoughts become more organized as trust/information increases

---

### Category 6: Emotional State & Moral Weight (Not Gamified)

**Witnessing Community Suffering:**
```
Church Conversation:
"She had a grandson. Marcus. And they took him."
"Her eyes. She's still hoping. Still thinking he might come back."
"I can't lie to her. I can't pretend I don't know."

Internal Conflict:
"Should I tell her? If she knows he's... down there... it will destroy her."
"But if I don't tell her, and I find him, she might never know."
"How do I make this choice? How do I decide what to do with someone's pain?"

Determination:
"I have to go down. I have to find him. Not just for her."
"Because they're still taking people. And it has to stop."
"Even if I die down there, at least I tried."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- No moral choice system (no "good/evil" button)
- Her thoughts reveal her values and commitments
- Actions have emotional weight that her monologue expresses
- Community relationships deepen through shared understanding

---

### Category 7: Environment & Spatial Awareness (Fog of War Thinking)

**Entering New Area:**
```
Street Level - The Veil:
"Storefronts. Jazz music somewhere. People I recognize."
"The fog of war around me. Everything beyond a certain distance becomes vague."
"But that's okay. I know The Veil. I can navigate the fading edges."

Covenant Hill (Outside The Veil):
"Everything is colder. Colors are wrong. Too blue."
"Surveillance. I can *feel* it. Cameras. Watching."
"The fog is different here. Not distance fog — it's institutional fog."
"I can see clearly but I'm being seen too clearly."
"I don't belong here. And everyone knows it."

Underground:
"Darkness. The fog is almost total. I can barely see five feet ahead."
"Stone walls. Infrastructure. Echoes of machinery."
"Is someone down here? Can they see me? I can't see them."
"This place feels wrong. It tastes like fear. My fear? Or residual?"
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Fog of war expressed through her perception of distance/clarity
- Different environments trigger different thought patterns
- She comments on what she can/can't see (communicates fog to player)
- Environment thinking reveals safety assessment

---

### Category 8: Decision-Making (No Tutorial, No Guidance)

**Facing Choice (No Markers, No Branching):**
```
Should I Take This Job?
"Store clerk job. Steady money. Safety. But it takes time."
"Time I could be using to investigate. Time people might be disappearing."
"But without money I can't survive. Can't sustain investigation."
"Work or explore. I can't do both fully."

Should I Trust This NPC?
"Church mother seemed genuine. But what if she's informant?"
"No. She was crying about her grandson. That wasn't fake."
"But people hide things. I hide things."
"She's taking a risk trusting me. I should take a risk trusting her."

Should I Go Underground?
"I know where The Drain is. I could go now."
"But I'm not ready. I don't have resources. I don't have allies."
"If I go unprepared I die down there. Alone."
"But waiting means they might move people. Might kill them."
"I need to be smart. I need to prepare. And I need to be ready to sacrifice."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- No UI indicates correct choice
- Her thoughts show *her* reasoning (player can disagree and choose differently)
- Monologue reveals she's weighing options, not being told what to do
- Consequences emerge from her choices, not from game judgment

## Thought Mechanics (Technical Implementation)

### Thought Frequency

**Varies Based on State:**
```
Calm/Safe (In Apartment):
- Sparse thoughts (1-2 per 10 seconds)
- Slower, reflective monologue
- Thoughts about memories, observations, plans
- Example: "It's quiet here. Good. I can think."

Active/Exploring:
- Moderate thoughts (1-2 per 5 seconds)
- Mix of observation and reaction
- Thoughts about environment, safety, direction
- Example: "Store's ahead. I need to look normal when I enter."

Danger/High Stress:
- Rapid thoughts (2-3 per 2 seconds)
- Fragmented, urgent, survival-focused
- Thoughts are scattered, incomplete
- Example: "Patrol. Don't run. Breathe. They're leaving. Are they?"

Sleeping/Dreaming:
- Monologue becomes disoriented, dreamlike
- Non-linear memory surfacing
- Trauma processing (nightmares)
- Example: "Stone walls. Pain. 'Asset approved.' What does that... where am I?"
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Thought density increases with stress
- Stress visible through *pattern* of monologue, not UI bar
- Player reads her state from thought quality and frequency

---

### Thought Presentation (UI/UX)

**Where Thoughts Appear:**
```
Screen Position: Top-left or center-top (non-intrusive)
Background: Slight semi-transparent background (readable but not dominant)
Font: Clean, readable, slightly italicized (distinct from dialogue)
Duration: Appears for 2-4 seconds (varies, player can't rewind)
Multiple: Can have multiple thoughts stacking briefly
```

**Example Visual:**
```
╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║  *My stomach. When did I eat last?*   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

[2-3 seconds pass, thought fades]

╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║  *There's that store. I could go.*    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝
```

**Cannot be Disabled:**
- Thoughts are required information (no UI option to turn off)
- This is intentional — forces player to engage with her perspective
- No way to "skip" thinking (you're in her head)
- Can't rewind thoughts (memory is unreliable)

### Thought Triggering System

**Automatic (No Player Control):**
```
Context-Triggered:
├── Location: Certain areas trigger specific thoughts
├── NPC-Triggered: Seeing/talking to NPC surfaces related thoughts
├── Item-Triggered: Seeing objects reminds her of things
├── Time-Triggered: Periodically surface ambient thoughts
└── State-Triggered: Hunger, fear, exhaustion trigger thoughts

Narrative-Triggered:
├── Quest-Triggered: Learning new information surfaces connected thoughts
├── Memory-Triggered: Trauma surfaces at certain stimuli
├── Relationship-Triggered: Deepening trust surfaces new worries
└── Consequence-Triggered: Major choices surface reflection

Random-Triggered:
├── Ambient: Random anxieties, observations, memories
├── Intrusive: PTSD thoughts (unwanted, disruptive)
├── Repetitive: Trauma = looping thoughts
└── Fragmented: Thoughts interrupted mid-way
```

**Examples:**

```
Location-Triggered (Church):
- Enters church
- Thought: "Safe. This place feels safe."
- Atmosphere triggers sense of spiritual safety
- NPC arrives: church_mother
- Thought: "Her eyes. She's looking for her grandson."
- NPC triggers emotional recognition

NPC-Triggered (Seeing Bureau Agent):
- Spots agent on street
- Thought: "Bureau uniform. Are they looking for me?"
- Instinctive fear response
- Agent walks past without reacting
- Thought: "They didn't see me. I'm okay. I'm okay."
- Relief followed by lingering paranoia

Item-Triggered (Seeing Surgical Equipment):
- Passes mechanic with visible tools
- Thought: "Tools. Sharp. Like the ones in the underground."
- Fragmented memory surface
- Thought: "Hands holding me down. No. No, that was... that was before."
- Distress/dissociation begins
```

### Monologue Patterns (Writing Style)

**She speaks in a specific voice. Consistent patterns show mental state.**

**Calm/Reflective:**
```
"The church was peaceful. Quiet. I could think there.
The church mother's grief was real. I could see it.
Maybe I should go back. Maybe she knows more about Marcus."
```
- Complete sentences
- Logical flow
- Forward-looking

**Anxious/Stressed:**
```
"Bureau. Two blocks. Are they looking?
No. Routine patrol. They always patrol.
But are they looking for ME?
Don't be paranoid. Be careful. Be smart."
```
- Fragmented
- Repetitive self-reassurance
- Circular logic

**Panicked:**
```
"RUN. Don't run. Don't attract attention.
They saw me. Did they see me?
Door. Need a door. Hide. Hide."
```
- Very short
- Urgent
- Action-focused
- Misspellings/broken grammar in some lines

**Dissociated:**
```
"I'm here. But I'm not. 
The world is... distance.
My hands are moving but I'm not... 
I'm not controlling them.
Where am I?"
```
- Existential questioning
- Loss of control
- Fragmented reality

**Traumatized (Memory Surface):**
```
"Stone walls. Where am I?
Lights. White lights.
Pain in my shoulders. They're holding me down.
'Servo calibration. Neural integration at 87 percent.'
No. No, that's not... that's not real.
That's not now."
```
- Disoriented
- Mixing past and present
- Confusion about what's real
- Emotional distress

### Early Game (Act 1: Waking Up)

**Themes:**
- Confusion about missing time
- Physical pain and cybernetic awareness
- Discovering she's been unconscious
- Basic survival (hunger, shelter, safety)
- No understanding of program yet

**Example Thoughts:**
```
"Where am I?"
"How long was I gone?"
"My arms hurt. Something's different about my arms."
"Someone left me water. Someone took care of me."
```

**Themes:**
- Piecing together what happened
- Building relationships, earning trust
- Understanding the program exists
- Connecting individual threads
- Growing determination to find people/truth

**Example Thoughts:**
```
"Marcus disappeared. Supply chains. Something's connected there."
"The store owner trusts me now. Maybe he'll tell me more."
"They were modifying people. I'm proof of that."

"I need the mechanic's trust to understand the modifications."
```

### Late Game (Act 3: Descent)

**Themes:**
- Preparing for confrontation
- Understanding scope of program
- Commitment to rescue/resistance
- Fear mixed with determination

```

"I'm not ready. But people are dying down there."

"If I wait too long more people die."

```

## Special Thought Sequences

### Nightmare Sequence (Processing Trauma)

During sleep, monologue becomes disoriented memory:

```
[Dream begins - non-linear, confusing]

"Underground. Stone. Cold."

"Hands. Too many hands holding me."

"'Left servo responding. Right servo calibrating. 
Neural integration satisfactory.'"

"Pain. Everywhere. Why can't I scream?"

"A face. I know that face. Why can I see... 
why can I only see their eyes above the mask?"

"'She'll be useful. Valuable. Asset.'"

"No. No, I'm not an asset. I'm... 
I'm..."

[Wakes]

"I'm awake. I'm home. That was... that was memory."

"How much of that was real?
It was real. All of it was real."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Nightmare thought sequences are non-interactive
- Player experiences disorientation
- Fragment memory surfaces (gives player information)
- Waking thought shows her processing

### Confrontation Moment (Making Choice)

When facing major decision with moral weight:

```
[Outside The Drain entrance]

"This is it. This is where I go down or I don't."

"If I go I might die. If I don't people definitely die."

"Church mother's grandson. How many others?"

"My legs are shaking. Is that fear or readiness?"

"I'm not ready. I'll never be ready."

"But I'm going anyway."

"Because no one else can. Because no one else will."

[Pause for player action - enter or turn back]

"I'm sorry. To everyone. To myself. For what comes next."
```

**Mechanical Integration:**
- Monologue leads up to choice point
- Her thoughts reveal her reasoning
- No UI prompts — pure thought
- Player understands her commitment through monologue

## Writing Guidelines for Thoughts

**DO:**
- Use first person (she's thinking, we hear her)
- Vary sentence length (stress changes syntax)
- Include physical sensations (what her body experiences)
- Mix present action with memories/worries
- Show uncertainty and doubt
- Repeat concerns (PTSD pattern)
- Use fragmentation when stressed
- Reference her cybernetics naturally
- Show her decision-making process

**DON'T:**
- Explain mechanics (no "I need 50 more currency to afford repairs")
- Use technical language (no "cybernetic servo calibration failed")
- Break character (she doesn't narrate like game tutorial)
- Tell player what to do (she thinks, doesn't instruct)
- Use perfect grammar in panic states
- Make her omniscient (she doesn't know everything)
- Be overly poetic (she's traumatized, not literary)
- Repeat exact same thought twice in a row

**[Opening - Waking Up]**

Thought: *"Where... where am I?"*

Thought: *"But how did I get here? Last thing I remember..."*

Thought: *"I don't remember. That's... that's not good."*

**[Moving to Kitchen]**

Thought: *"Water bottle. Someone left water. And the fridge..."*

*[Opens fridge - mostly empty]*

Thought: *"Nothing. There's nothing. But I was fed. Someone fed me."*

Thought: *"I'm hungry. Really hungry. How long since I ate?"*

**[Looking in Mirror / Noticing Body]**

Thought: *"What happened to my arms?"*

Thought: *"The cybernetics are... they're different. Newer. Smoother than I remember."*

Thought: *"They hurt. Everything hurts."*

Thought: *"They were working on me. Whoever brought me here... they modified me?"*

*[Touching arms]*

Thought: *"Or... someone else did this. Before. When I was..."*

Thought: *"I can't remember. My brain won't let me remember."*

**[Finding Money]**

Thought: *"Money. Someone left money."*

Thought: *"Enough for food. Maybe enough for a few days."*

Thought: *"So I can survive. For now."*

**[Decision to Leave]**

Thought: *"I need to go outside. I need food."*

Thought: *"But I don't want to go outside. It's been so long."*

Thought: *"Everything will be watching. Everyone will know I've been gone."*

Thought: *"But I have to eat. And I'm alive, which means I can do this."*

**[Walking to Door]**

Thought: *"Just walk. Just like normal. Like you belong here."*

Thought: *"But I'm shaking. Can they see that I'm shaking?"*

**[Stepping Outside]**

Thought: *"The Veil. I'm back in The Veil."*

Thought: *"Everything looks the same but I'm different. I'm not the same person who left."*

Thought: *"What did they do to me down there?"*

**[Walking to Store]**

Thought: *"One foot. Then the other. Don't think too much. Don't panic."*

Thought: *"Store's right there. I know that place. I can do this."*

Thought: *"Just buy food. Just talk to the owner like normal. Like nothing happened."*

Thought: *"Like I didn't disappear. Like I wasn't... modified. Like I'm not hunted."*

*[Approaching store]*

Thought: *"Here we go. Be normal. Be calm. Be invisible."*

This is your Deus Ex / Elysium-style thought system. It's the **only tutorial the player gets**. The game communicates entirely through her fragmented, damaged, honest thoughts about what's happening.

The player never reads: "Quest Started" or "Objective: Find Food" or "Follow the Marker"

Instead, they read her thoughts and understand what matters through *her* understanding, at *her* pace, with *her* emotional weight.
