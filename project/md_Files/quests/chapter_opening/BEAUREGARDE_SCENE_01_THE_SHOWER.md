# BEAUREGARDE — SCENE 1: THE SHOWER (Opening Scene)

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Opening scene. Player's first interaction. Defines character psychology for entire game.

---

## Opening Visual

```
FADE IN on darkness.
Sound: Water running at full blast. Loud. Aggressive.
Her breathing. Labored. Wet.

Camera slowly focuses.

She's standing in a cramped shower stall.
Water cascades over her. Ice cold.
Fully clothed. Jeans, shirt, hoodie soaked through.
Her body shaking violently.

She can FEEL something on her. Tentacles wrapped around her torso.
But in the darkness and confusion, she can't see what it is.
She doesn't know what it is.

Water pools at her feet. Dirty. Grey.

VISUAL DETAILS (Redlining/Poverty):
Shower stall is small. Tile cracked. Mold visible in corners.
Faucet old, corroded. Water pressure broken (too strong).
Bathroom walls thin. Sound of neighbors' voices audible through them.
Single bare bulb overhead. Flickering.

The apartment visible through open bathroom door:
Studio. Bare. One window boarded up.
Mattress on floor.
No furniture except one chair.
Radiator in corner (broken, no heat).

OUTSIDE SOUNDS:
Sirens distant.
Neighbors arguing through walls.
Traffic. Horns.
Sounds of a struggling neighborhood.
```

## First Awareness (She Becomes Conscious)

```
She's conscious but disoriented.
Something is on her body.
She can FEEL it.
Warm. Moving slightly. Alive.
But she can't see it.

The water is COLD and LOUD.

EMOTIONAL ZAP hits her: Alien distress.
Not her distress.
Something ELSE's distress.
Raw. Panicked. Desperate.

It floods her nervous system.
Moment of overwhelming panic that isn't entirely hers.

She shakes harder.
```

## THE CHOICE (Player Must Make It)

```
INTERNAL DIALOGUE WINDOW OPENS

Competing thoughts appear immediately:

FEAR (Red, sharp, jagged):
"It's cold. You're freezing. Get out. Turn it off."

BODY (Grey, urgent):
"Hypothermia risk. Immediate. Core temperature dropping."

SURVIVAL (Orange, directive):
"Stop the water. Preserve heat. Now."

But then:

SOMETHING ELSE floods her mind.
Not a thought.
A FEELING.
Alien. Desperate. Drowning.

The DISTRESS from whatever is on her intensifies.

The shower water—the thing NEEDS it.
She can FEEL that need.
Like hunger but for water.
Like drowning in air.
Like suffocation.

And because it's on her nervous system, SHE feels it too.

New thoughts appear:

INSTINCT (Green, urgent, not clear if it's HER instinct):
"No. No. It needs the water. Don't turn it off."

CONFUSION (Purple, loud, NEW):
"What is on you? What needs water like that?"

DENIAL (Grey, defensive):
"I don't know what that feeling was. It wasn't mine. Where did it come from?"

FEAR (Red, spiking):
"Something is ON your body. Something ALIVE. Something can make you FEEL its needs directly."

CURIOSITY (Purple):
"Your nervous systems are integrated. Direct emotional transfer. That's how it communicates."

THE CHOICE APPEARS:

[PROMPT: The water is cold. You're hypothermic. But something on your body NEEDS that water. What do you do?]

CHOICE A: TURN THE WATER OFF
"Survival first. Stop the cold. Whatever's on you can adapt."

CHOICE B: LEAVE IT RUNNING
"The thing on you needs this. You can endure the cold. Leave it."

CHOICE C: TURN IT DOWN (Compromise)
"Reduce the flow. Less cold for you. Some water for... whatever this is."

CHOICE D: IGNORE THE CHOICE
"Can't decide. Stand here. Let someone else choose."
```

# CHOICE A: TURN THE WATER OFF

## What She Does

```
FEAR (Red): "It's COLD. You're FREEZING."
BODY (Grey): "Hypothermia. NOW."
SURVIVAL (Orange): "TURN IT OFF."

She reaches up with shaking hand.
Grabs the faucet handle.
Turns it. Hard.

Water STOPS.

Silence is immediate. Jarring.

She stands in wet clothes. Dripping.
Breathing hard.
The cold intensifies now that the water isn't moving.

Her hand is still on her head/shoulder.
She feels that SOMETHING shift.
Movement. Tension. Like panic.
But she still can't see it.
She doesn't know what it is.
```

## The Alien Distress (It Gets Worse)

```
The moment water stops:

DISTRESS FLOODS HER MIND.

Not gradual.
FLOODS.

Like drowning but without water.
Like suffocation without air.
Like panic but ALIEN.
Like dying and asking for help in a language she doesn't understand.

It's in her nervous system.
In her body.
But it's NOT her.

FEAR (Red, HER fear, spiking):
"What IS that? What just happened?"

BODY (Grey):
"Something moved. On your head. On your shoulder. You felt it panic."

But she FEELS the panic.
It's not separate.
It's flooding through her like her own heartbeat.

CONFUSION (Purple, NEW, LOUD):
"That wasn't a thought. That was FEELING. And it came from inside you."

She touches her head slowly.
Her fingers brush against something.
Tentacles.
Multiple.
Warm.
Alive.
Moving in distress.

But she can't see it.
Can't understand it.
Can only FEEL it screaming internally.

DENIAL (Grey):
"I'm hallucinating. I'm in shock. Hypothermia causes hallucinations."

FEAR (Red):
"No. No you FELT that. Something is ON you. Something ALIVE."

INSTINCT (Green, URGENT, NOT clear whose):
"Turn the water back on. NOW. It's drowning. Please. PLEASE turn the water back on."

But is that HER instinct?
Or is that the THING on her, begging through her own nervous system?

She can't tell.
```
