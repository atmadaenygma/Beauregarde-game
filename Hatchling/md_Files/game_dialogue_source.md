## SHOWER SEQUENCE (branching)

*Player makes a choice about the water. Four branches, each teaching something different about the symbiosis.*

**Prompt:** "It's cold. You're freezing. Get out. Turn it off."

Voices in the sequence:
- "Hypothermia risk. Core temperature dropping."
- "Stop the water. Preserve heat. Now."
- "No. It needs the water. Don't turn it off."
- "What is on you? What NEEDS water like that?"
- "Something is ON your body. Something ALIVE. Something that can make you FEEL its needs directly."

### Branch A — Turn off water

> "Water stops. That alien panic FLOODS your nervous system. Not your fear — its fear. Immediate. Drowning."
> "Turn it back on. NOW. It's dying. Please. PLEASE."
> "It wasn't your choice. You don't owe it anything."

**Sub-branch A1a — turn back on:**
> "Water touches it — the distress STOPS. Not gradually. Like a switch."
> "It needs water. You need it alive more than you need to deny reality. That's enough for now."

**Sub-branch A1b — leave it off:**
> "You're choosing not to help something that's suffering. That makes you responsible."
> "You push the distress to the background. It's still there. Like a cry you refuse to answer."

**Sub-branch A1c — compromise:**
> "Neither satisfied. Both alive. The creature settles into something livable."
> "This is how you survive together. Constant negotiation."

**Sub-branch A1d — dissociate:**
> "You leave your body. The creature's distress continues but you're watching from outside."
> "Your body forces you back. Cold. Gasping. The distress floods you again."
> "Presence means pain. Absence means death. You're trapped between them."

### Branch B — Leave it running

> "You're getting colder. But the alien distress has stopped."
> "It needed the water. You stayed in the cold for it. It knows that now."
> "A woman. In water. She understood this. She chose the creature's survival too."

### Branch C — Turn it down

> "Water reduced. Temperature stabilizing. Its panic shifts from drowning to manageable."
> "Neither of you gets everything. But neither of you dies. That's partnership."
> "You have responsibility to something now. That changes everything going forward."

### Branch D — Do nothing (frozen / indecisive)

> "You don't move. The cold advances. Your body makes the choice without you — medium flow."
> "You can't escape responsibility through inaction. Your body's choices ARE your choices."
> "Everything you do or don't do has consequence."

## MIRROR SEQUENCE (branching)

*In the bathroom. First time Octavia can see the octopus.*

**Sub-branch A1 — Try to pull it off:**

## VIDEO LIBRARY (MOVE historical footage — Charlie's archive)

### "THE DAY POLICE DROPPED A BOMB ON PHILADELPHIA"

**Subtitle:** *Philadelphia, May 13, 1985 — A City Government Bombed Its Own Neighborhood*

**Status:** [SUPPRESSED]

> Philadelphia, 1985. A city government dropped a military bomb on a residential block. 11 people died — 5 of them children. 61 homes burned. The fire commissioner said let it burn. The only person who went to prison was someone who survived. Nobody else was charged.

---

**Timeline:**

**1973** — John Africa founded MOVE. A Black naturalist and revolutionary organization. Anti-industrial. Anti-government. Communal living, strict veganism, animal rights. They raised children and animals together. The city called them dangerous.

**1978** — A year-long armed standoff ended in gunfire. Officer James Ramp was killed. Nine MOVE members — the MOVE 9 — were arrested and sentenced to 30 to 100 years. Defense testimony placed MOVE in the basement at the time. Ramp was shot at a downward angle from above.

**1982** — The remaining members relocated to a row house in a middle-class Black neighborhood. To protest the imprisonment of the MOVE 9, they built a fortified rooftop bunker and broadcast political messages through loudspeakers, day and night.

**May 12, 1985** — Neighbors were evacuated from their homes the night before.

**May 13, 1985** — After a day-long assault — 10,000 rounds of ammunition, tear gas, water cannons — a state police helicopter dropped a military-grade satchel bomb onto the rooftop bunker. The fire commissioner was ordered to let it burn. It burned for one hour and thirty minutes before firefighters were called.

**The toll:** 11 killed. 5 children, ages 7 to 14. 61 homes destroyed. 250 people homeless. $33 million in taxpayer funds spent rebuilding defective homes.

**Two survived:** Ramona Africa and Michael Ward (Birdie Africa), 13 years old. Birdie was pulled from a water-filled alley with severe burns. His mother, Rhonda Africa, died in the fire.

**Aftermath:** Ramona was arrested while fleeing the burning building — cuffed despite her burns. She was the only person tried for May 13. She served seven years. No one who ordered the bombing was ever charged.

---

**Quotes:**

> *"Nobody was ever arrested for the murder of my family. Nobody went to jail but me."* — Ramona Africa

> *"The house shook. We start feeling the heat and the smoke — we knew there was a fire. We heard the crackling of the trees."* — Ramona Africa

> *"People ought to take the lesson from me and stand up for what is right. As long as we can fight, we're going to fight."* — Ramona Africa
