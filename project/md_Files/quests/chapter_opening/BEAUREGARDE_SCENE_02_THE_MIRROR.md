# CHOICE A: LOOK IN THE MIRROR

```
She stops in front of the mirror.
Looks.

Sees her face.
Sees TENTACLES on her head.
Red. Living. Real.

She reaches up slowly.
Her fingers touch one of the tentacles.

It's warm.
It's ALIVE.
It responds to her touch.

A new thought appears:

"Can I... take it off?"

[SUB-CHOICE A1: TRY TO PULL IT OFF (Aggressive)]
[SUB-CHOICE A2: TOUCH IT GENTLY (Explore)]
[SUB-CHOICE A3: LEAVE IT ALONE (Back Away)]
```

---

## SUB-CHOICE A1: TRY TO PULL IT OFF (Aggressive)

```
She grabs one of the tentacles.
PULLS.

HARD.

The tentacle RESISTS violently.
It doesn't come off.

Instead:

PAIN explodes in her scalp.
Like ripping skin.
Like tearing nerve endings.

But also:

The OCTOPUS DISTRESS floods her mind INSTANTLY.
PANIC. AGONY. TERROR.

But not just fear.
RAGE.

The octopus is FIGHTING back.

The tentacle she's pulling GRIPS her wrist.
HARD.
Not gently anymore.
FIRMLY.
Almost painfully.

It's restraining her.
Holding her in place.
Communicating through FORCE:
"STOP. You're hurting US."

HORROR (Red): "It's in pain! You're hurting it!"
BODY (Grey): "PAIN. Nervous system screaming. Stop!"
RAGE (Orange, octopus RAGE): "Don't DO that again. EVER."

But the rage isn't entirely hers.
It's the octopus's rage bleeding through.
It's ANGRY that she tried to remove it.
That she tried to TEAR it away.

She releases the tentacle.
Pulls her hand back.

The octopus releases her wrist.
But slowly.
Making sure she understands:
"I can hurt you too. Don't make me."

MEMORY (Dark blue): "They're integrated. Merged. You can't separate them without destroying both of you."

She steps back from the mirror.
Her wrist ACHING where it was gripped.
Her scalp BURNING where she tried to pull.

ACCEPTANCE (Soft blue, grim, fearful): "It's part of you. Trying to remove it would kill you both. And it WILL defend itself."

GRIEF (Deep blue): "How long have I had this? Since when am I like this?"

[TRACKING STATS:
+ SELF-AWARE-OF-PERMANENCE
+ OCTOPUS-COMMUNICATION-ESCALATED (it can hurt her back)
+ MUTUAL-PAIN (both suffering)
+ OCTOPUS-DEFENSIVE (now protective of itself)
+ DESPERATION (wants escape, realizes there isn't one)
- HOPE-FOR-SEPARATION
+ TRAPPED-FEELING
+ FEAR-OF-CREATURE
+ DAMAGED-TRUST (octopus learned she wants to remove it)]

Thought: "It won't come off. And it will fight me if I try again."

Scene transitions to apartment.
```

## SUB-CHOICE A2: TOUCH IT GENTLY (Explore)

```
She reaches up.
Touches one of the tentacles.
Very gently.

The tentacle is WARM.
Slightly wet.
Muscular.
Moving under her fingers.

It IMMEDIATELY responds to her touch.

The tentacle RELAXES into her touch.
Curls slightly around her finger.
Like holding her hand.

The octopus sends a wave of... RELIEF.
Not just recognition.
RELIEF that she's touching it.
That she's willing to make contact.

EMOTION FLOODS HER (Not a thought, just FEELING):
Warmth. Recognition. Joy (alien joy, but JOY).

INSTINCT (Green): "It's been waiting for this. For you to touch it. For you to acknowledge it."

She runs her fingers along the tentacle.
Feels the suckers.
Feels them GRIP her fingers gently.
Not pulling.
Just... holding.

The octopus is ACTIVELY engaging with her touch.
Responding.
Communicating through sensation.

She pulls her hand back slowly.
The tentacle FOLLOWS her hand.
Not letting go immediately.
Holding on for an extra moment.
Like saying:
"Wait. Don't leave. Stay with me."

Then it releases.
Falls back to her head.

But the CONNECTION is there now.
Both of them aware.
Both of them PRESENT.

ACCEPTANCE (Soft blue): "It's not a weapon. It's not a curse. It's... alive. And it wants to be with you."

MEMORY (Dark blue): "How long have I had this? How long have we been together? Do I remember when they..."

The question trails off.
The memory won't surface.
But the question matters.

She needs to know HOW LONG.

[TRACKING STATS:
+ OCTOPUS-BONDING (conscious, deliberate, MUTUAL)
+ EMPATHY-FOR-CREATURE (it NEEDS her)
+ RECOGNITION-OF-OTHER (it's conscious, it WANTS)
+ PARTNERSHIP-FOUNDATION (it sought her contact)
+ EMOTIONAL-CONNECTION
+ SELF-PORTRAIT-UNLOCKED (she can see herself now)
+ TIME-MISSING-AWARENESS (how much time is gone?)]

Thought: "It wanted me to touch it. How long has it been waiting?"

Scene transitions to apartment.
```

---

## SUB-CHOICE A3: LEAVE IT ALONE (Back Away)

```
She looks at the tentacles in the mirror.
She can see them MOVE.
She can feel them on her head.

But she doesn't touch them.
She doesn't pull.
She doesn't reach up.

She just... backs away from the mirror.

FEAR (Red): "Don't touch it. Don't engage. Just leave it alone."

DENIAL (Grey): "If you don't interact with it, maybe it stays separate."

She steps back.
Eyes still on the mirror.
Creating distance.

The tentacles in her reflection IMMEDIATELY respond to her withdrawal.

They TENSE.
They CURL inward.
They PULL AWAY from where they were reaching toward her.

The octopus SENSES her rejection.
And it RETRACTS.

OCTOPUS-DISTRESS (SHARP, SUDDEN, CLEAR):
The emotion floods her mind:
HURT. REJECTION. ABANDONMENT.

It's not manipulative.
It's HONEST.
The creature is FEELING what she's feeling—rejection—and responding with its own pain.

GUILT (NEW, OVERWHELMING):
"You're hurting something that was just trying to connect with you."

INSTINCT (Green, conflicted):
"It WANTS you. And you're rejecting it. How is that fair to something that has no choice?"

She turns away from the mirror.
Walks to the apartment door.

But the tentacles on her head are TENSE now.
WITHDRAWN.
HURT.

She can FEEL the change in them.
The mood shift.
The emotional distance.

MEMORY (Dark blue): "How long have I had this? How long have we both been suffering?"

The question echoes as she walks away from the mirror.

[TRACKING STATS:
+ DENIAL-MAINTAINED
- OCTOPUS-BONDING (actively rejected)
+ SEPARATION-SUSTAINED (at cost)
+ GUILT-CARRYING (for rejecting it)
+ OCTOPUS-HURT (it learned she doesn't want it)
+ BARRIER-FORMED
+ SELF-PORTRAIT-UNLOCKED (she can see herself now)
+ TIME-MISSING-AWARENESS (how much time is gone?)]

Thought: "I hurt it. How long have we both been like this?"

Scene transitions to apartment.
```

# CHOICE B: WALK PAST

```
She walks past the mirror without looking.
Never even stops.

Deliberate avoidance.

DENIAL (Grey): "One problem at a time."

She doesn't see her reflection.
She doesn't touch the tentacles.
She doesn't engage.

But she KNOWS it's there.
The mirror is calling.
Behind her.
Waiting.

The octopus on her head shifts.
She can feel it moving.
Reaching slightly.
Like trying to get her attention.

But she keeps walking.

[TRACKING STATS:
+ DENIAL-MAINTAINED
- SELF-AWARENESS
- SELF-PORTRAIT-LOCKED
+ MIRROR-AVAILABLE-LATER
+ CONSEQUENCE-THROUGHOUT-GAME
+ OCTOPUS-CONFUSED (why won't she look? why won't she touch?)]

Thought: "One problem at a time."

Scene transitions to apartment.
```

## Summary: The Mirror Choice

**CHOICE A1: PULL IT OFF (Aggressive)**
→ Discovery of permanence, mutual pain, octopus defensive, fear of creature, damaged trust, escape impossible

**CHOICE A2: TOUCH IT GENTLY (Explore)**
→ Mutual recognition, octopus communicates need, partnership begins, empathy forms, connection deepens

**CHOICE A3: LEAVE IT ALONE (Back Away)**
→ Guilt, octopus hurt, rejection communicated, barrier formed, separation sustained at emotional cost

**CHOICE B: WALK PAST**
→ Avoidance, no self-awareness, mirror remains unresolved, octopus confused, denial maintained
