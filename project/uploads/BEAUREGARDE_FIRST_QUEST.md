# BEAUREGARDE — First Quest: Waking Up

## The Opening Loop (Hour 0-1)

This is the player's first experience. No tutorials, no guidance. Just her thoughts and the world responding.

---

## Scene 1: The Apartment (Confusion)

**Where She Is:**
- Small apartment bedroom
- Her belongings scattered (clothes, personal items, photos)
- Sunlight through window (morning or afternoon?)
- Calendar on wall (days are crossed off — she's been here a while)
- Water bottle on nightstand (someone cared for her while unconscious)

**What Happens:**
She wakes up. No cutscene, no explanation. Just opens her eyes.

**Her Inner Monologue (What Player Sees):**

*"Where... am I?"*

She sits up. Pain. Her body hurts — cybernetics are active, wrong somehow.

*"My arms hurt. Both of them. The servos are hot."*

She looks around. This is her room. She knows that. But when did she get here?

*"I remember... the walls are mine. The photos. That's my jacket. But how long?"*

Calendar on wall. Days crossed off. One week. Two? Hard to count.

*"I've been sleeping. Someone... someone left water. Food in the fridge?"*

She tries to stand. Legs are shaky. Muscle memory is there but timing is wrong.

*"My balance is off. The cybernetics are responding slow. They need recalibration."*

Window shows The Veil outside. Buildings she recognizes. But she doesn't remember coming home.

*"I don't remember. I don't remember the trip here. I don't remember the last week."*

Growing panic (not shown with UI, shown through her thoughts):

*"Who brought me here? Who kept me alive? Why can't I remember?"*

She steadies herself on the wall.

*"Okay. Okay. First things first. I'm alive. I'm home. I'm safe."*

But her heart is beating faster (RED turning toward YELLOW). The stress is showing.

---

## Scene 2: The Apartment (Assessment)

**Where She Is:**
- Still in bedroom, but now moving through it
- Notices details she missed while panicking

**Her Inner Monologue:**

She looks at the calendar more carefully.

*"Seven days. I've been unconscious for seven days."*

She checks her body. Bandages. Scars. Surgical marks.

*"They opened me up. They worked on the modifications. My arms... my arms are different. The servos are smoother. Too smooth. New?"*

She touches her face. Her eyes. Memory fragments surface but don't stick.

*"There's something I'm supposed to remember. Something bad. But my brain won't... it won't let me see it clearly."*

Protective dissociation kicking in — she knows she experienced trauma but can't access the full weight of it yet.

*"That's probably good. If I remembered everything right now I'd..."*

She doesn't finish the thought.

She walks to the kitchen (small apartment, just off the bedroom).

*"Food. I need to eat. When did I last eat?"*

The fridge is mostly empty. Some leftovers (moldy). Bread on counter (stale). Nothing really edible.

*"Nothing fresh. Someone fed me while I was sleeping but... there's no food now."*

Her stomach confirms it. Empty. Aching.

*"I'm hungry. Really hungry. Like I haven't eaten in days."*

She checks the small table by the window. Money. Small pile of cash.

*"Someone left this. Left it here for me."*

She counts it (not specific amount, just registers it):

*"Enough to buy food. Enough to survive a few days if I'm careful."*

Growing realization:

*"I have to leave. I have to go outside. Get food. I haven't been outside in a week and I..."*

Her heart rate increases (YELLOW). The thought of leaving is causing stress.

*"I can do this. It's just The Veil. It's safe. People know me here."*

But she's not sure they do. Not really. Not if she's been missing a week.

*"Okay. I'll go to the corner store. I know the corner store. It's close. I can get bread, maybe something hot. And come back."*

She gets dressed (shows the player her sprite can move, interact with objects).

---

## Scene 3: Leaving the Apartment (Dread)

**Where She Is:**
- Apartment hallway
- Door to her unit behind her (safety point)
- Stairs leading down
- Sounds from other units (neighbors living their lives)

**What Player Does:**
Navigate down the stairs (simple left-right movement, maybe up-down to go down stairs).

**Her Inner Monologue:**

As she leaves the unit:

*"Okay. Out of the apartment. Into the building."*

Hallway is quiet. Early afternoon maybe. Most people are at work.

*"I recognize this hallway. The paint. The smell. Cooking from unit 3C — that's always Mrs. Chen."*

She passes a neighbor (older woman, carrying laundry).

Neighbor looks at her. Recognizes her.

**Neighbor says:** "Oh honey, good to see you up and about. You were out for so long."

**Inner monologue (in response):**

*"She knows me. She knows I've been gone. How long did everyone know? Was I just... missing?"*

*"I should say something back. Act normal."*

But she just nods. Scared to speak. Can't remember what to say.

Neighbor keeps walking (unbothered, used to seeing her around).

She continues downstairs.

*"She acted like it was normal. Like people disappear for a week and come back and it's... okay?"*

This realization hits different:

*"Unless... unless people disappear all the time here. Unless that's expected. Unless no one questions it."*

She reaches the building entrance. Looks outside.

*"The Veil looks the same. The buildings. The signs. The fog boundary's over there where it always is."*

But her stomach is tight. Heart racing slightly (YELLOW).

*"Why am I scared of going outside?"*

She forces herself through the door.

---

## Scene 4: The Streets of The Veil (Uncomfortable)

**Where She Is:**
- Street level, outside her building
- Storefronts visible
- NPCs going about daily life (other residents, vendors, people doing business)
- Jazz music from somewhere (club or street musician)
- Morning/afternoon light, warm colors

**What Player Does:**
Move right (toward corner store, which is 2-3 buildings away).

**Her Inner Monologue:**

First step outside hits different:

*"Air. Fresh air. I'd forgotten what this smells like. Cooking. Neon. Someone's perfume."*

*"It's warm. The Veil is always warm but... it feels like home."*

But something is off:

*"Why am I noticing all this? Why does it feel strange? I grew up here. I know this place."*

She moves down the street. NPCs pass her. Most don't react. Some nod. One man calls out.

**Man says:** "Yo, she's back! Thought you got snatched!"

**Inner monologue (realizing):**

*"Snatched. He said snatched. Like disappearing is... something that happens. Something expected."*

*"How many people disappear? How many people are just... gone?"*

This thought sits heavy but she pushes it down.

She keeps walking.

*"Corner store. Two more buildings. I can see it from here. The blue awning. That's it."*

Her cybernetics are active now, warmed up. Movement feels better.

*"My arms are working better. The servos are responsive. Someone did good work on them. But why? Why would they modify me if they were going to let me go?"*

Confusion and dread mixing:

*"Unless I wasn't let go. Unless I escaped. Unless..."*

She doesn't finish that thought.

*"No. I'm in The Veil. I'm safe. I'm home. That's all that matters right now."*

The corner store is getting closer.

*"Food. Then home. Then I can sleep. Then I can think about what happened."*

But her heart is still elevated (YELLOW). Something in her knows the truth but her mind isn't ready to access it yet.

---

## Scene 5: The Corner Store (Vulnerability)

**Where She Is:**
- Small neighborhood corner store
- Warm lighting, familiar
- Store owner behind counter (older man, kind face)
- A few other customers (regulars, locals)
- Shelves with food, drinks, basics
- Radio playing softly

**What Player Does:**
Enter store. Move to shelves. Gather food (bread, can of soup, drink, maybe fruit).

**Her Inner Monologue (Entering):**

*"I'm inside. The store. It's smaller than I remember but... it's the same."*

Store owner looks up. Sees her. Recognition flickers.

**Store owner says:** "Welcome back! Haven't seen you in a while. How you doing?"

**Inner monologue (responding):**

*"He's asking like it's casual. Like I just went on a trip. Not like I disappeared."*

*"Maybe I did just go on a trip. Maybe the missing week is something else. Maybe—"*

But she looks in the mirror/reflective surface and sees her arms. The new cybernetics. Fresh surgical marks barely healed.

*"No. This isn't a trip. This is something that was done to me."*

She browses shelves. Reaching for food.

*"Food. Focus on food. That's real. That's manageable."*

She grabs bread (necessary):

*"Bread. How many days will this last if I'm careful? Three? Four?"*

She grabs a can of soup (comfort):

*"Something hot. Something that feels like someone cared."*

She grabs water (survival):

*"Water. Always water. In case I need to hide. In case I need to run again."*

She grabs something else (maybe fruit, maybe candy — small comfort):

*"I deserve one thing that's not just survival. One thing that's just... nice."*

Moving to counter, her heart is racing now (YELLOW becoming more pronounced):

*"Why am I so nervous? It's just buying food. It's just the corner store."*

But she's aware of eyes on her. The other customers. The store owner. Like she doesn't belong here right now.

*"Am I being watched? Are they looking at the cybernetics? Are they wondering where I've been?"*

Hypervigilance kicking in (trauma response):

*"I shouldn't have come here. Everyone knows me here and that means everyone knows I've been gone. Everyone is wondering. Everyone is watching."*

She places items on counter.

**Store owner says:** "You good? You seem tense."

**Inner monologue:**

*"He noticed. He's kind but he noticed. How obvious am I being?"*

*"I just survived something. I don't know what. I don't know how. And now I'm standing in a corner store being watched and my heart won't stop racing."*

She pays (money exchanges, transaction completes).

**Store owner says:** "Take care of yourself. Eat something good."

**Inner monologue (leaving):**

*"He means well. He's being kind. But his words sound like a warning. Like he knows something bad is happening. Like I should be careful."*

She leaves the store.

---

## Scene 6: Back Outside (Panic Building)

**Where She Is:**
- Street level
- Back the way she came
- Same NPCs, same buildings, same sounds
- But everything feels different now

**What Player Does:**
Move left (back toward her apartment building).

**Her Inner Monologue:**

*"Get home. Get inside. Lock the door."*

She's moving faster now. Not running (doesn't want to attract attention) but moving with purpose.

*"Something is wrong. Something is very wrong and I don't know what it is."*

Her heart is definitely YELLOW now — visible pulse, faster rhythm, anxiety visible on her face.

*"I'm being hunted. I don't know who by but I'm being hunted. I can feel it. The way that man on the street looked at me. The way the store owner warned me. They know something."*

*"Or maybe I'm paranoid. Maybe the week of unconsciousness has scrambled my brain. Maybe I'm just scared because I don't remember."*

*"Either way, I need to be inside. I need to be safe."*

She passes different streets now. Notices details:

*"There's a Bureau patrol two blocks over. Or someone in uniform, anyway. I can hear their radios. Are they looking for me? Did I do something? Am I in danger?"*

Survival instinct driving now:

*"Don't run. Don't look at them. Don't be memorable. Just walk home like everything is normal."*

She's almost there.

*"One more block. My building is right there. I can see it. The fire escape. The window. That's my window."*

---

## Scene 7: Inside the Apartment (Safety, Temporary)

**Where She Is:**
- Back in her apartment
- Door locked behind her
- Safe

**What Player Does:**
Move to window or bed. Sit down. Rest.

**Her Inner Monologue (Finally Safe):**

*"Door is locked. I'm inside. No one can get me here."*

She collapses on bed or sits by window.

*"My heart is still racing. It's taking a long time to come down. The panic is real but there's nothing here. I'm safe."*

She looks at the food she bought.

*"I can eat now. I have food. For a few days I won't be hungry. I can sleep. I can rest. And tomorrow... tomorrow I can think about what happened."*

But the hunger is immediate. She eats (bread, soup maybe if she heats it up — player can choose).

*"This is better. Food makes everything better."*

Her heart starts to come down (YELLOW, but trending toward RED as she settles).

*"Why was I so scared out there? It's The Veil. It's home. Everyone there knows me."*

Realization:

*"Or maybe that's exactly why I should be scared. Because everyone knows I disappeared. And they're all wondering. And there are people looking for me. And..."*

She stops herself.

*"Not now. Sleep first. Then nightmares. Then I can deal with reality."*

She lies down.

---

## Scene 8: Sleep (First Nightmare - Memory Fragment)

**Where She Is:**
- In her apartment, sleeping
- But experiencing a nightmare/memory

**What Happens:**
Player loses control. Character enters dream sequence. Everything is disorienting, non-linear, painful.

**What Player Sees (First Nightmare):**

*Darkness. Stone walls. Cold.*

*"Where am I?"*

*"Hands. There are hands holding me down. I can't move my arms."*

*"Pain. In my shoulders. In my wrists. In my... in my chest?"*

*"Someone is talking. A voice. Clinical. Professional. Like this is routine."*

*"' Left servo responding well. Neural integration at 87%. Ready for final calibration.'"*

*"Fear. Pure fear. Like every cell in my body is screaming."*

*"But I can't scream. Something in my throat. Something preventing sound."*

*A face appears. Blurry. Masked. Eyes visible above the mask. Cold.*

*The person says something but it's muffled:* *"'She'll be useful. Valuable property. Asset approved for deployment.'"*

*Pain spikes. White hot. Everywhere.*

*Then black.*

---

## Scene 9: Waking From the Nightmare (Understanding Begins)

**Where She Is:**
- Back in apartment, in bed
- Gasping, covered in sweat
- Heart pounding (YELLOW, almost BLUE)
- But awake now

**Her Inner Monologue (After Nightmare):**

*"It was real. That was real. That wasn't imagination."*

*"I was in a place. Underground. Stone walls. Clinical. And they were... they were modifying me. Deliberately. On purpose."*

*"They called me 'property.'"*

*"I'm not remembering a nightmare. I'm remembering what happened."*

Her heart is racing (BLUE territory):

*"I need to breathe. I need to calm down. I need to—"*

She forces herself to breathe slowly.

*"Okay. Okay. That was days ago. That was when I was unconscious. That was... before."*

*"And now I'm here. I'm home. I'm safe. Someone got me out. Someone saved me."*

But the fear is bone-deep now:

*"If they did that to me, and they let me go, they'll come back. They'll come back for me. I'm valuable property. Why would they just let me go?"*

She gets up. Drinks water. Looks out window at The Veil.

*"Unless I wasn't supposed to leave. Unless I escaped. Unless someone had to risk something to get me out."*

*"And if I escaped, they're still looking. Right now. They're still searching."*

She looks at her arms. The cybernetics. New, sophisticated, humming softly with power.

*"And they built this into me. This is inside me. What if they can track it? What if they know where I am?"*

Panic returning (BLUE):

*"No. No. I'm overthinking this. The nightmare is real but the immediate danger isn't. Not right now. Not today."*

She lies back down.

*"Tomorrow I'll think about this. Tomorrow I'll figure out how to survive. Tomorrow I'll find out who saved me and why."*

*"Tonight I just need to sleep. Even if there are nightmares, I need rest."*

She closes her eyes.

## Quest Complete: The First Loop

**What the Player Experienced:**

1. **Waking up confused** — understanding she's been unconscious
2. **Realizing she's alone** — someone took care of her but it's unclear who
3. **Physical hunger** — actual survival need
4. **Forced to leave safety** — apartment no longer enough, need food
5. **Emotional overwhelm outside** — hypervigilance, noticing she's been missing
6. **Transaction with store owner** — slight human connection but also dread
7. **Panic return home** — surviving outside is taxing
8. **Safety in familiar space** — apartment is a refuge
9. **Sleep and nightmare** — trauma surfaces, memories return fragmented
10. **Waking understanding** — she's been modified, she's property, she's hunted

**What the Player Learned About Her:**

- Her thoughts are the only UI (player reads her emotional/physical state through monologue)
- She's hypervigilant (she notices patterns, dangers, social cues)
- She's intelligent (she pieces information together, even while scared)
- She's traumatized (her brain dissociates to protect her, memories surface in fragments)
- She's resourceful (she survives, she gets food, she gets home)
- She's alone (no one helped her on the street, no allies revealed yet)
- She's hunted (the nightmare is real, she was experimented on, someone is looking for her)

**What the Player Understands About The Veil:**

- It's familiar and safe but people disappear
- Disappearances are common enough that neighbors don't overreact
- There's institutional presence (Bureau, Watch patrols)
- People know each other but are cautious about asking questions
- There's an underground of missing people (implied)
- There's a resistance or support system (someone saved her)

**What Happens Next:**

- She's alive, she's home, she has food for a few days
- She's traumatized but functional
- She has questions but no answers
- She'll need to go back out eventually (food runs out, she needs information)
- The next quest will be: repeat the process, but maybe talk to someone, maybe go to a different location, maybe take the first job

## Key Design Notes for Implementation

**No Tutorial, No Guidance:**
- Player never told they need food
- Player never told to leave apartment
- Player discovers these needs through her inner monologue
- World responds naturally (store exists, food available, no UI prompting)

**Inner Monologue As UI:**
- All information flows through her thoughts
- Her stress level visible through thought patterns (shorter sentences = panic, longer = reflection)
- Player reads her physical state (heart color) and emotional state (monologue) together

**No Quest Markers, No Objectives:**
- "Find food" is never stated as an objective
- Player discovers it because she's hungry
- Corner store is just a place that exists (she knows where it is)
- Everything is organic discovery, not guided gameplay

**Permanent World State:**
- Calendar shows time passing
- Neighbor remembers her disappearance
- NPCs treat her like someone who's been gone
- Her appearance shows trauma (new cybernetics, surgical marks)

**Single Save File:**
- Everything that happens stays happened
- If she had gone somewhere else first, that changes what she finds
- Player is making choices with permanent consequences from moment one
